# Paper Triage Ranking Rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 3:

- `relevance`: direct fit to the user's profile and research question.
- `innovation_potential`: likelihood of suggesting a new angle, method, assumption, or theory.
- `proof_pattern_value`: likelihood of yielding reusable proof-pattern SkillCards.
- `positioning_value`: importance for literature positioning or novelty claims.
- `access_readiness`: open PDF, user PDF available, or metadata only.
- `reading_cost`: inverse score; 3 means cheap to read, 0 means high cost.

Suggested grouping:

- `must_read`: high relevance plus high proof-pattern value or direct positioning risk.
- `should_read`: useful for methods, innovation, or background but not immediately blocking.
- `maybe_read`: interesting but speculative, redundant, or low immediate value.
- `skip`: weak relation, benchmark-only with little value, duplicate, or inaccessible without need.

Always explain the group decision in one or two concrete sentences.
