# Innovation Signals

Use these labels to classify why a paper may be useful for innovation:

- `new_proof_technique`: introduces a proof move, decomposition, potential, duality, coupling, or inequality route that may transfer.
- `sharper_bound`: improves rate, constants, dimension dependence, sample complexity, regret, stability, or approximation error.
- `weaker_assumption`: proves a similar conclusion under weaker regularity, convexity, smoothness, distributional, or structural assumptions.
- `cross_domain_transfer`: uses a method in a neighboring domain that could be adapted to the user's problem.
- `lower_bound`: provides impossibility, optimality, minimax, or separation results that clarify what can be improved.
- `new_abstraction`: reframes the problem using a new object, geometry, operator, coupling, or variational principle.
- `alternative_formulation`: changes the problem statement, loss, metric, relaxation, or proof target in a potentially useful way.

For each candidate, ask:

- What exact idea could transfer?
- What assumptions would break the transfer?
- Does this affect the user's main problem, proof method, or positioning?
- Is it worth reading now, skimming, or saving for later?
